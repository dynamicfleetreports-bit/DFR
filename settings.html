Exchange Rates

Used to show foreign-currency pricing on your Info Pack. Enter how many Rand equal 1 unit of each currency — look this up on any forex site (e.g. xe.com) and update whenever rates move meaningfully. Your Info Pack's foreign prices are calculated live from your Rand pricing above ÷ these rates, so they stay in sync automatically.

Admin Master PIN

Lets you open ANY partner's portal (clients, commission, sub-partners) using this PIN instead of their own personal one — so you can check what's happening with each partner without needing to know every individual PIN. Keep this private; anyone with it can view every partner's financial data. Viewing a portal this way does not count as a login in that partner's activity log.
